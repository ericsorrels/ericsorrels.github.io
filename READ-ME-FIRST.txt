THE GRAY MAN — YOUR WEBSITE
===========================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------

  content.js        ← ALL the words on the website. This is the only
                      file you ever need to open.

  index.html        the main site (hero, about, music, news, contact)
  access.html       the password-protected Early Digital Access page

  assets/audio/     ← put your album tracks here (see the note inside)
  assets/downloads/ ← put your PDFs and album zip here (note inside)
  assets/img/       ← put your key-art ocean photo here (see below)
  assets/css/       the design. Ask Claude before touching this.
  assets/js/        how the site works. Ask Claude before touching this.


TO SEE THE SITE ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
------------------------------------

Double-click index.html and it opens in your browser.

One catch: opened this way, some browsers won't load the text from
content.js, so you may see the built-in placeholder text instead.
That's normal and it will look correct once the site is online. To
preview it properly, ask Claude to "start the preview server."


TO CHANGE ANY TEXT
------------------

1. Open content.js in TextEdit (right-click → Open With → TextEdit).
2. Change only what's between the "quotation marks."
3. Save, then refresh your browser.

Leave the commas, colons, and brackets alone — those are the wiring.
If something breaks, ask Claude to fix content.js.


THE EARLY DIGITAL ACCESS PAGE
-----------------------------

Address:   your-website.com/access.html

Give that link and password to supporters. The page stays unlocked
for the rest of their browsing session, then locks again.

IMPORTANT — WHAT THIS PASSWORD DOES AND DOESN'T DO:

  It works like a "members only" sign on a door. It keeps the page
  out of Google and stops casual visitors from wandering in.

  It is NOT a bank vault. Someone technical who really wants your
  files can get past it, because everything on a website is
  ultimately downloadable. This is true of any password on a simple
  site like this one, not just yours.

  So: it's perfect for early access, thank-you materials, and
  supporter downloads. If you ever need to protect something
  genuinely valuable (say, a full score you're licensing), tell
  Claude and we'll set up real protection.

To change the password, ask Claude — it isn't stored as plain text
in any file, so it can't be changed by hand.
