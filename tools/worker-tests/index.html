Vault worker — stand-in tests

Runs cloudflare/vault-worker.js exactly as it is on disk, against a made-up guest list, a made-up Stripe, a made-up Gumroad and a made-up mail relay. Nothing here reaches the real ones, and nothing here is part of the site. Open it through the preview server: python3 tools/preview-server.py 8423, then localhost:8423/tools/worker-tests/. It takes about twenty seconds, most of it two tests that wait on purpose.
