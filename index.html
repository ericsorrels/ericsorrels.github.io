About the Show

They say that when a great storm is coming, a lone figure in gray appears on the beach at dusk — walking the dunes, warning anyone who will listen to leave the island. Those who see him are spared.

Drawn from the folklore of the South Carolina coast, The Gray Man is a new musical about love, loss, and the storms that return for us — told across three hurricanes, and the years between them.
