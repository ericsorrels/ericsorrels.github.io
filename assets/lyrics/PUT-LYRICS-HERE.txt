HOW TO ADD LYRICS
=================

Each song's words go in this folder, named by its track number — the
same numbers as the audio files:

      12.lrc     timed words. The line lights up as it is sung, and a
                 listener can click any line to jump the song there.

      12.txt     plain words. Shown as they are, with no timing.

If a track has both, the timed one is used. If it has neither, the
panel simply says there are no lyrics for that track, and nothing looks
broken.

Plain words are just the song typed out, one line per line, with a
blank line between verses.


MAKING A TIMED FILE
-------------------

Use the tap-along tool on your computer:

      tools/lyric-timer.html      (double-click it)

Load the song and its plain words, press play, and tap the space bar as
each line begins. When you're done it hands you the finished file, named
for you. Move that file into this folder.


AFTER ADDING OR CHANGING ANY FILE HERE
--------------------------------------

Open content.js, find "lyrics_version" under "access", and raise the
number by one. Browsers keep a copy of everything they have already
read; this is what tells them to fetch the new words instead.


WORTH KNOWING
-------------

Like the album audio, anything in this folder can be read by anyone who
knows its address, password or not.
