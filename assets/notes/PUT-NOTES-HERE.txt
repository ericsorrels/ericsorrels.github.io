HOW TO ADD LINER NOTES
======================

Whatever you want to say about a song — where it came from, who played
on it, what it is really about — goes in this folder, named by its
track number, the same numbers as the audio and the lyrics:

      12.md

It appears under the "Notes" tab at the top of the lyrics panel, beside
"Lyrics", and follows whichever track is playing.

If a track has no file here the panel simply says there are no notes
for it, and nothing looks broken. You can write notes for one song and
leave the rest until later.


WRITING ONE
-----------

A .md file is an ordinary text file. TextEdit will do — but use
Format > Make Plain Text first, or it saves a .rtf instead. Name it
12.md, not 12.md.txt.

Just type. A blank line starts a new paragraph:

      We cut this one in a single take, at two in the morning,
      with the windows open.

      Nobody planned the ending.

Press Return once and the words move to a new line but stay in the
same paragraph. Press it twice for a new paragraph.


MAKING WORDS STAND OUT
----------------------

      *one star*         italic
      **two stars**      bold
      ***three stars***  both

      # A heading        a small heading
      ## A heading       a smaller one

      ---                a dividing line, on a line of its own

Anything else you type comes out exactly as you typed it.

ONE THING TO WATCH. In content.js, putting *stars* around the show's
name is what makes it read as THE GRAY MAN in capitals. In here, stars
mean italics instead — the ordinary writing kind. It is the same
symbol doing a different job in a different place. If you want the
show's name in capitals in a note, just type it in capitals.

Underscores are left alone in here, so a file name or an address with
_underscores_ in it stays exactly as you wrote it.


AFTER ADDING OR CHANGING ANY FILE HERE
--------------------------------------

Open content.js, find "notes_version" under "access", and raise the
number by one. Browsers keep a copy of everything they have already
read; this is what tells them to fetch the new words instead.


WORTH KNOWING
-------------

Like the album audio and the lyrics, anything in this folder can be
read by anyone who knows its address, password or not. Write these for
the people you let in, but don't put anything in them you would mind a
stranger reading.
