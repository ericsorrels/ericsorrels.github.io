HOW TO ADD A VIDEO
==================

1. Put your video file in this folder (assets/video), whatever it's
   called and whatever format it's in.

   Use the version you exported BEFORE posting to Instagram, not a
   copy downloaded back from Instagram — Instagram squeezes the
   quality down when you upload, and that damage can't be undone.

2. Ask Claude to prepare it for the site. A video straight out of an
   editor is usually far too big to stream comfortably, and is often
   in a format some browsers can't play at all. Claude makes a small
   web copy (an .mp4 beside your original), picks a cover image, and
   adds it to content.js under "videos".

3. Your original stays on your computer. Files ending in .mov in this
   folder are never published, so a big master can't go online by
   accident. If your original is an .mp4 instead, move it out of this
   folder once the web copy is made — .mp4 files here DO get published.

CHANGING WHAT'S SHOWN
---------------------

Everything about each video — its caption, the credit line, its cover
image, and which comes first — lives in content.js under "videos",
with notes explaining each line.
