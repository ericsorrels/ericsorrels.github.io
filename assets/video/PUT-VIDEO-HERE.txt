HOW TO ADD YOUR TEASER VIDEO
============================

1. Put your video file in this folder (assets/video) and name it:

      teaser.mp4

   Use the version you exported BEFORE posting to Instagram, not a
   copy downloaded back from Instagram — Instagram squeezes the
   quality down when you upload, and that damage can't be undone.

   The file must be .mp4 to play everywhere. If yours is .mov or
   something else, tell Claude and it can be converted.

2. Open content.js and find the "video" section under "music".
   Set "shape" to match your video:

      "wide"    a normal landscape video
      "square"  as tall as it is wide
      "tall"    a vertical phone video

   Instagram posts are often square or tall, so check this — it's the
   one setting that matters for how the video sits on the page.

3. Refresh the site.

WHILE YOU DON'T HAVE A FILE HERE
--------------------------------

The video simply doesn't appear. The Music section looks complete
without it, so nothing looks broken or half-finished.

OPTIONAL: A COVER IMAGE
-----------------------

By default the video shows its own first frame before someone presses
play. If that frame isn't flattering, save a still image in
assets/img/ and write its name in content.js under "poster", like:

      poster: "assets/img/teaser-cover.jpg",
